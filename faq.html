A few helpful notes

Frequently asked questions

Have another question? Reach out and we'll be happy to help.

Are your products handmade?

Yes. Knots & Bloom products are presented as handmade crochet pieces. Small differences in color and shape are part of handmade work.

Can I request a custom color or design?

Use the custom order form to share your idea. We will confirm feasibility, timing, and pricing before making begins.

How do I care for crochet flowers?

Keep them dry and dust gently. Care needs vary by yarn and construction; contact us if you need item-specific guidance.

When will my order ship?

Production and delivery timing depends on the item and destination. We'll confirm the estimate with you before an order is finalized.

How does checkout work?

The current checkout sends an order request only. Payment and shipping are confirmed separately; no online payment is taken on this site.
